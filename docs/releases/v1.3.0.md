## 简体中文

### 新功能

- **实体背包容量也能自定义**：可以设置大型 6 级背包的容量，小型、中型和其他等级的背包会由程序一起调整，方便搭配自己的玩法。
- **背包容量变化提前看**：可以选择背包类型、等级和驮骡技能等级，直接查看有多少格可用、多少格仍有负重，减少反复进游戏试配置的麻烦。
- **两种预览背包容量的方式**：游戏模式模拟游戏中实际看到的背包效果；预览模式用标记清楚显示实体背包增加的容量。
- **新增背包容量数据详情窗口**：集中查看各等级驮骡加成，以及小型、中型、大型背包各等级增加的格数，方便比较和选择。

### 修复与体验改进

- **修正游戏和预览不一致的问题**：装备或卸下背包后，可用格数和负重区域显示更准确，方便判断当前配置是否够用。
- **改善大容量死亡背包的显示**：捡回死亡掉落的背包时，可通过滚动查看物品，减少背包界面超出屏幕、影响拾取的问题。
- **减少旧模组冲突**：安装新配置后，可勾选清理本工具生成的其他背包模组，换容量时更省心。
- **界面更清爽**：采用蓝色配色和更清晰的图形，改善按钮、输入框和提示；中英文使用相同布局。

### 默认配置与启动

- **默认配置回到原版数值**：人物背包 40 格、初始免负重 32 格、大型 6 级实体背包 48 格，可以从熟悉的配置开始调整。
- **单文件程序更轻便**：减小下载体积，并加快后续启动，日常调整背包更方便。

## English

### New features

- **Customize equipped backpack capacity**: You can set the capacity of a large quality-6 backpack. The tool adjusts small, medium, and other quality levels together to suit your playstyle.
- **Preview backpack capacity changes**: Choose a backpack type, quality, and Pack Mule rank to see how many slots are unencumbered or still encumbered, without repeatedly entering the game to try settings.
- **Two ways to preview backpack capacity**: Game mode simulates how your backpack looks in the game. Preview mode uses markers to clearly show the extra slots provided by an equipped backpack.
- **New backpack capacity details window**: Compare Pack Mule bonuses and the additional slots provided by every quality of small, medium, and large backpacks in one place.

### Fixes and improvements

- **Fixed differences between the preview and the game**: Available slots and encumbered areas now display more accurately when you equip or remove a backpack, helping you decide whether your setup has enough space.
- **Improved large death-backpack display**: Scroll through items when retrieving your dropped backpack, reducing problems with oversized inventory windows getting in the way of looting.
- **Fewer conflicts with old configurations**: After installing a new setup, you can remove other backpack mods generated by this tool, making capacity changes easier.
- **A cleaner interface**: A blue color scheme, sharper graphics, and improved buttons, inputs, and messages. Chinese and English now use the same layout.

### Defaults and startup

- **Vanilla starting values**: Defaults are now 40 character slots, 32 starting unencumbered slots, and 48 slots for a large quality-6 backpack, so you can start from familiar settings.
- **A lighter single-file app**: A smaller download and faster subsequent launches make everyday backpack adjustments more convenient.
